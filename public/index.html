Our Journey
From "Time Pass" to Joe's Brew

It all began in 2006 with a small dream and a big hope. We opened our first coffee stall, “TIME PASS” — more than just a shop, it was a home where friendships blossomed, conversations flowed, and memories were brewed.

For two precious years we served coffee and shared smiles until challenges forced our doors to close in 2008. Yet the dream never died. It simply waited to rise again.
