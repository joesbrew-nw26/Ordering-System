## Project

**Joe's Brew In-House Ordering & Brand Website**

A mobile-first digital menu and table-ordering web application paired with a brand showcase for **Joe's Brew** (Estd 2006). Customers scan QR codes at physical tables to browse the curated menu, customize items, and place orders directly to the ground-floor kitchen from both ground and terrace seating, while kitchen staff track and advance tickets in real time via an interactive Kitchen Display System (KDS).

**Core Value:** Eliminates ordering friction for terrace customers by routing digital table orders and waiter calls directly to the ground-floor kitchen with instant real-time sync.

### Constraints

- **Mobile-First UX**: 95% of ordering traffic is on smartphones; design must be thumb-friendly and not rely on desktop hover states.
- **Styling**: Vanilla CSS with customized design tokens and bento-box styling; no Tailwind.
- **Real-Time Communication**: Zero-refresh order routing between terrace customer phones and kitchen display via WebSockets / event streaming.
- **Payment Localization**: High priority on Indian UPI payment flows (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM) along with cash/counter options.
